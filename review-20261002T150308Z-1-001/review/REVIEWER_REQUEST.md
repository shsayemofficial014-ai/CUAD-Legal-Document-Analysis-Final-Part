# Request to a reviewer (copy, paste, send)

Subject: 30 minutes of your time — reviewing a contract risk rating scale

Hi [name],

I'm finishing my undergraduate thesis at BRAC University. It's a system that reads a
commercial contract and flags which clauses a signer should pay attention to.

Part of it is a rating scale: for each of 41 standard clause types (non-compete,
uncapped liability, governing law, and so on) we assigned a risk level — High, Medium
or Low — from the point of view of the party with **less bargaining power**, meaning
the side that did not draft the agreement.

The problem is that three computer science students wrote that scale, and none of us
has legal training. My supervisor has asked me to get it looked at by someone who does
before I submit.

**What I'm asking:** go through the attached sheet (41 rows), and for each one just
mark whether you agree with the level we assigned. Where you disagree, write the level
you'd give and, if you have a moment, one line on why. About 30 minutes.

**What I'll do with it:** report the results in the thesis exactly as they come back,
including the disagreements — those are the useful part. I'll credit you by name and
role if you're happy with that, or keep it anonymous ("reviewed by a final-year law
student") if you'd rather.

Two things worth saying up front so you're not misled:
- The level applies to the clause **type**, not to specific wording. It's saying
  "contracts with a non-compete deserve a second look", not "this non-compete is harsh".
- I'm not asking for legal advice and nothing here will be presented as such. It's a
  sanity check on whether a CS student's intuitions about contract risk are reasonable.
