\section*{Known determinism caveats}
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=2pt]
  \item MPS kernels are not bit-reproducible across \texttt{torch} versions; a rerun should reproduce reported scores to roughly three decimal places, not exactly.
  \item ROUGE-L in \texttt{summ\_pilot.py} is computed by an inline longest-common-subsequence implementation (matching \texttt{test.ipynb}) rather than by the \texttt{rouge\_score} package, so the two are not interchangeable.
  \item The retrieval-ceiling measurement of Section~\ref{sec:ceiling} was computed over all 510 contracts before the split was drawn (Section~\ref{sec:threats}).
\end{itemize}
